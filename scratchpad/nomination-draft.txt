Add what you paid and when, and ThingCost shows the cost per day — that $1,000 phone is really $2.74/day, and dropping. Our signature Worth It Score (0–100) weighs price, ownership time, and how often you actually use each item, so buyers finally see whether a purchase earned its keep.
